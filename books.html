Hemmer

Professor Dr JG Hemmer is a reclusive specialist in the area of genomics. His story is not his to tell. Dive into a thrilling near-future science fiction adventure through a dystopian vision so close to our world you might see your house from there. From Rotterdam to Wolfsburg Germany, through the Earth-bound Tyson Station all the way to Venus, we have a chance to integrate into a variety of cultures along the way. What is the core conflict between Earth and the Spacers? Who struck first and why? What kind of weapons do you use in a pressurized space ship? Will Jamie ever find a job? Hemmer is a fast-paced and funny scifi action adventure from acclaimed author Robert Willnow, clocking in as the fourth installment in the Written By Victors series.
