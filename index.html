How working with ZingLab goes

  1. You tell us the problem. Not the solution — the problem. An email is fine. Half the time the best answer is smaller than you expected.
  2. We tell you the truth. What it'll take, what it'll cost, and whether you'd be better off with something off the shelf. We'd rather lose a project than sell you one you don't need.
  3. We build it in the open. Working versions early and often, so you're reacting to something real instead of a slide deck.
  4. You own all of it. Code, accounts, documentation. No hostage situations. And we stick around afterward, because software is never actually done.

Why hire a lab instead of an agency?

You work with the builders

The people who scope your project are the people who build it. Nothing gets lost in a hand-off, because there isn't one.

Marketing brain, engineering hands

We've launched campaigns that landed on the Amazon homepage. We know what software has to do for the business, not just how to build it.

Private and fast by default

Our own apps run in the browser and keep user data on the user's device wherever possible. Your customers will notice the speed. Your lawyers will notice the privacy.

Search and AI ready
