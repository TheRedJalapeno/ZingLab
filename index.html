How working with me goes

  1. You tell me the problem. Not the solution — the problem. An email is fine. Half the time the best answer is smaller than you expected.
  2. I tell you the truth. What it'll take, what it'll cost, and whether you'd be better off with something off the shelf. I'd rather lose a project than sell you one you don't need.
  3. We build it in the open. Working versions early and often, so you're reacting to something real instead of a slide deck.
  4. You own all of it. Code, accounts, documentation. No hostage situations. And I stick around afterward, because software is never actually done.

Why hire a lab instead of an agency?

You work with the builder

The person who scopes your project is the person who writes it. Nothing gets lost in a hand-off, because there isn't one.

Marketing brain, engineering hands

I've run campaigns that landed on the Amazon homepage. I know what a site has to do for the business, not just how to build it.

Private and fast by default

My own apps run in the browser and keep user data on the user's device wherever possible. Your customers will notice the speed. Your lawyers will notice the privacy.

Search and AI ready

Clean markup, structured data, sitemaps, and llms.txt, so both search engines and AI assistants understand what you do. This page is a decent example.
