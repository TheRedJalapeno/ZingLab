What is ZingLab?

ZingLab is the LLC our founder uses for all of his esoteric projects.

One month it is writing science fiction novels, next it is patenting and manufacturing screwdriver hex shank adapters, and then partnering with research institutions to make an early warning system for dementia symptoms.

Polymath playground is one description, another is semi-retired ADHD wonderland.
