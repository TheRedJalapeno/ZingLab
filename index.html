Targeted Microbiome Research for Animal Health

We turn ambition into action to achieve uncommon results.

ZingLab is an independent biomedical research laboratory investigating the therapeutic potential of commensal microbial communities in companion animal populations.

Our work focuses on the intersection of oral microbiology, probiotic science, and veterinary health outcomes. We source, culture, and characterize microbial strains with the goal of developing evidence-based, microbiome-targeted interventions for underserved patient populations in companion animal medicine.

ZingLab operates with the rigor of academic research and the agility of an independent laboratory. We maintain active research projects, collaborate with suppliers and institutional partners, and are committed to transparent, reproducible science.
