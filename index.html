Targeted Microbiome Research for Animal Health

ZingLab is an independent biomedical research laboratory investigating the therapeutic potential of commensal microbial communities in companion animal populations.

Our work focuses on the intersection of oral microbiology, probiotic science, and veterinary health outcomes. We source, culture, and characterize microbial strains with the goal of developing evidence-based, microbiome-targeted interventions for underserved patient populations in companion animal medicine.

ZingLab operates with the rigor of academic research and the agility of an independent laboratory. We maintain active research projects, collaborate with suppliers and institutional partners, and are committed to transparent, reproducible science.

Predicting Dementia Risk — Before Symptoms Begin

In collaboration with researchers at Loughborough University, ZingLab developed one of the most accessible early-onset dementia risk tools available today: a simple, at-home digital assessment that takes minutes to complete and requires no clinical setting, no personal data, and no prior medical history.

The assessment, based on Loughborough University research, is designed around validated cognitive indicators and asks only for a participant's age, then generates a meaningful risk categorization based on their response time. Users who opt in for follow-up can share an email address alongside a small set of optional demographic data points; everyone else participates fully anonymously. No personal data is sold, shared, or retained beyond what participants explicitly choose to provide.

The results have been striking. The tool has demonstrated strong effectiveness in stratifying risk across demographic groups, and the growing dataset of anonymous, researcher-accessible responses is already informing new lines of inquiry into early cognitive decline. It is a prime example of what ZingLab does best: take rigorous science and put it directly in the hands of the people who need it.
