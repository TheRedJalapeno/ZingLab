## Where the competition is genuinely good

- **Merriam-Webster** is the right call when you need **authoritative usage notes** and precise distinctions between near-synonyms.
- **Power Thesaurus** shines for **modern, informal, and niche** vocabulary thanks to its community voting.
- **WordHippo** is useful when you also need **rhymes, antonyms, and translations** in one place.
- **Thesaurus.com** has a deep catalog and plenty of **example sentences**.
