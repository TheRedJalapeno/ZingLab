Risk is a great game with two famous problems: **it takes forever**, and **dice decide too much**. Everyone has a story about the one defending army that held off an invasion of twenty. Online versions fix the setup time but usually keep both problems.

[To The Victor](https://tothevictorgame.com/?ref=zinglab-blog) started as a family board game in 2014 — a mashup of *Settlers of Catan*, *Risk*, and *Munchkin* — built to fix exactly those things. Now it's online.

## The contenders

- **[To The Victor](https://tothevictorgame.com/?ref=zinglab-blog)** — "The Game of Total Global Conquest," online multiplayer from ZingLab.
- **Risk: Global Domination** — the official licensed digital Risk.
- **[Warzone](https://www.warzone.com/)** — free, highly customizable Risk-like with thousands of maps.
- **[Conquer Club](https://www.conquerclub.com/)** — long-running turn-based online Risk community.
- **[Catan Universe](https://www.catanuniverse.com/)** — official digital Catan, for the trading-and-building side.

## Why To The Victor wins

### 1. No dice. No waiting. No excuses.

That's the tagline, and it's the design. Without dice, every loss is a decision you can learn from, and every win is yours. It also removes the slowest part of physical Risk: rolling, rolling, and rolling again.

### 2. A real game in 30 minutes

A typical match runs about **30 minutes**. That's a lunch break, a group call, or "one more game" that actually is one more game.

### 3. Four ways to win

Pure domination makes the end of Risk a slow mop-up. To The Victor offers **four distinct victory paths**, so a player who is behind on territory is never truly out of it — and the leader can never relax.

### 4. Built for groups

Start a war, share the **room code**, and friends join instantly — 2 to 6 commanders. There's in-room **chat**, a **"Call a vote"** button for table decisions, a mute toggle, and fullscreen mode.

### 5. More than Risk

The original board game mixed Catan's resource building, Risk's conquest, and Munchkin's gleeful betrayal. The result plays like none of them, and that's the point.

## Where the competition is genuinely good

- **Risk: Global Domination** is the pick if you want **official Risk rules** and a big matchmaking pool.
- **Warzone** is unbeatable for **custom maps and huge games** with dozens of players.
- **Conquer Club** suits players who like **slow, asynchronous** turn-based games over days.
- **Catan Universe** is the official home of Catan if trading and building is what you love.
