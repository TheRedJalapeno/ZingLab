After a hailstorm, a roofing company's biggest enemy usually isn't the competition. It's its own reps knocking the same doors on the same streets while the next neighborhood over goes untouched. Two reps work Maple Street, nobody works Oak, and the homeowner on Maple gets three knocks and gets annoyed.

Most canvassing software was built as a sales CRM first, with a map added later. That works well for managing a pipeline, but less well for answering the question a storm manager actually has at 7am: **which streets have we already worked?**

## Why LeadAlign wins for storm crews

### 1. You can see the work, not just the workers

Seeing where each rep is right now is useful. Seeing **every street each rep has already driven, painted on one map in their color**, is what actually stops duplicate knocks. A manager can split a neighborhood in seconds, and a rep can tell at a glance where to go next.

### 2. One button, zero training

A new rep on day one doesn't need a CRM. They need to drive, spot a damaged roof, and tap **Lead here**. Notes and statuses can be added later. If a storm brings in a dozen temporary reps, they're productive the same morning.

### 3. It works where the storm hit

Storm zones often have weak or no cell signal. LeadAlign's trails, pins, and territory alerts keep working offline and **sync automatically when signal returns**, and areas a rep has already viewed stay on the map. The screen stays on while the phone is charging, so the trail keeps recording on the dash.

### 4. Territories that enforce themselves

Give each rep a territory and LeadAlign **alerts them when they leave it or drift into a teammate's**. No add-on fee, no arguments in the parking lot.

### 5. Pricing a crew owner can say yes to

**$29 per rep per month, every feature, month-to-month.** Add seats when a storm hits and remove them when it's over. Try it for 14 days without a credit card. There's no multi-year contract and no implementation fee.

### 6. It plays well with your other tools

LeadAlign isn't trying to be your CRM, contract tool, or payroll. Export leads as a **spreadsheet, KML, or GeoJSON**, or send a set of leads straight to **Google Maps as ordered stops** for a closer or tree crew.

## Where the competition is genuinely good

- **SalesRabbit** is built for **large, multi-office door-to-door operations** that want CRM, pipeline, and canvassing in one system and are ready for a longer contract and a formal implementation.
- **SPOTIO** fits **enterprise field sales organizations** with complex reporting requirements and the budget for a dedicated implementation manager.
- **Badger Maps** is strong for **B2B reps routing to existing accounts**, such as distributors, medical sales, and service visits, rather than sweeping new streets.
- **Spreadsheets and paper maps** are fine for one or two reps on familiar ground. Past that, the duplicate doors cost more than the software.
