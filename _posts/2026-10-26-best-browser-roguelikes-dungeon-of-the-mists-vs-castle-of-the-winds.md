Roguelikes have a reputation: brutal, ASCII, and installed from a zip file you found on a forum in 2004. Many of the greats still look like that — and they're still great. But if you want to **open a tab and descend into a dungeon in ten seconds**, on a laptop or a phone, the field narrows fast.

We made [Dungeon of the Mists](https://openfantasy.org/?ref=zinglab-blog) for exactly that, as a love letter to *Castle of the Winds*. Here's how it compares.

## The contenders

- **[Dungeon of the Mists](https://openfantasy.org/?ref=zinglab-blog)** — tile-based browser roguelike from ZingLab.
- **Castle of the Winds** — the 1989 Windows classic (released as freeware in 1998) that inspired Mists.
- **[Shattered Pixel Dungeon](https://shatteredpixel.com/)** — a beloved open-source roguelike for mobile and desktop.
- **[Brogue: Community Edition](https://github.com/tmewett/BrogueCE)** — elegant, deep, and famously tense.
- **[Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup](https://crawl.develz.org/)** — huge, deep, and playable online via WebTiles.
- **[NetHack](https://www.nethack.org/)** — the ancestor, still unmatched in sheer interactions.

## Why Dungeon of the Mists wins (for browser play)

### 1. Zero friction

No download, no emulator, no account to start. Open [openfantasy.org](https://openfantasy.org/?ref=zinglab-blog), make a character, descend. That matters more than people admit: the best roguelike is the one you actually start.

### 2. Designed for touch, not ported to it

Tap a monster to attack, tap empty space to cancel. **Rest, Spells, Items, Attack, Inventory** live on a single action bar. It plays as well on a phone on the bus as on a desktop.

### 3. The Castle of the Winds feel, modernized

Tile-based dungeons, spells and mana, salvageable gear, and a camp you can **recall** to between dives — the comfortable rhythm of the classic, with **progressive difficulty** that ramps as you go deeper.

### 4. A world worth reading

Mists has creepy, recoverable **lore** and **companions with real dialogue**. Most roguelikes treat story as optional garnish; Mists makes it a reason to go one floor deeper.

### 5. Cloud saves and leaderboards

Sign in and your character follows you between devices. Climb the leaderboard. Optional cosmetic add-ons let you support the game without anyone getting a gameplay edge.

## Where the competition is genuinely good

- **Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup** and **Brogue** are deeper, more punishing tactical puzzles. If you want hundreds of hours of mastery, play them too.
- **Shattered Pixel Dungeon** is a superb mobile app with short, satisfying runs.
- **NetHack** remains the encyclopedia of "the game thought of that?"
- **Castle of the Winds** itself is worth playing for history — if you're willing to set up an old Windows environment.
