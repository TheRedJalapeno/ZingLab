Robert Willnow is a pen name for Robert Allen. 

I've been writing forever but only sat down for long form creative works in 2024 during a sabbatical. My work is meant to create strong, clever, kind role models for my daughter, with the goal of having a substantial body of work by the time she is able to read. 

My characters are quirky and realistic, the science is sound, and scenarios crafted with care. Some setups in book one will pay off in book 5. At any moment there are dozens of loose threads, just like real life. You'll never know when someone is on a parallel path to your own, unless you look out the window.
