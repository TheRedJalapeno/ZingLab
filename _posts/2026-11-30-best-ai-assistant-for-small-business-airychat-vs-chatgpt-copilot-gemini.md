Most people stare at an empty chat box and don't know what to ask. AiryChat ships **ready-made assistants with distinct roles** — a general assistant, an analytical thinker, a marketer, a software developer, and a UI/UX designer — so employees who are new to AI get useful results on day one.

### 2. Multi-model by design

AiryChat is built on **OpenAI, Meta, and Google** services. As those models improve, you benefit — without migrating your team or renegotiating a platform contract.

Draft documents. **Scan contracts for specific clauses.** Ask questions of PDFs, spreadsheets, and Word files. Condense large data sets. Turn a question into a web search and get a synthesized answer. Generate images. Talk to any assistant **voice-to-voice**.

Personal and business accounts don't mix, and long-term memory keeps conversation history useful without starting from scratch each time.

Want an AI assistant for your customers or staff that carries **your name, not ours**? AiryChat offers **white-label** solutions. The big platforms don't.

- **ChatGPT Business** is a strong general-purpose option if you only need OpenAI models and prefer to build your own custom GPTs.
