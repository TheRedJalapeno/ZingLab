## Why RingFence wins

### 1. The whole world, not just US ZIP codes

Postal codes in **120+ countries**, including the US, Canada, Mexico, the UK, and 26 of the 27 EU member states. Towns in **250 countries and territories**. A 100 km radius around Strasbourg returns French *and* German results, and a search near Detroit includes Windsor. Plenty of tools stop at the border.

### 2. Every town, even without a postal code

Many places, especially rural ones and outside the US, aren't cleanly covered by postal codes. RingFence **includes town names even where no postal code exists**, so your invite list or franchise document doesn't have holes in it.

### 3. No silent guessing

Type "Springfield" and RingFence shows a **picker** instead of choosing one for you. Center on a postal code (02139, SW1A, 10115, M5V), a town ("Lyon, France"), or **exact coordinates**. The circle is drawn on the map as you type the radius.

### 4. Ready for where the list goes next

Results come **nearest first**. You can **filter and edit** them, then **copy** the postal codes as a list for your ad platform, or export all rows or only filtered rows to **CSV or Excel** for Marketo, Pardot, Eloqua, HubSpot, a CRM, or a spreadsheet.

### 5. Built for automation and AI

The same search runs through a **REST API** with an OpenAPI spec, and through a remote **MCP server** that gives AI agents `radius_search` and `resolve_location` tools. Your agent can ask "which towns are within 30 miles of each store?" and get real data back instead of guessing.

### 6. Accurate, and flat-priced

Distances are **great-circle** measurements to each postal code's or town's center point, boundary inclusive. They're accurate at any latitude, across the 180° meridian, and across borders. Pricing is **$99/month with no per-search charges**, so nobody has to ration lookups.
