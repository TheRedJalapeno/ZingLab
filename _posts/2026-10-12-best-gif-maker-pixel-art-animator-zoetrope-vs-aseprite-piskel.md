## Where the competition is genuinely good

- **Aseprite** is still the gold standard if pixel art is your full-time job and you want a native desktop app with a deep scripting ecosystem. Zoetrope plays nicely with it — palettes and sprite-sheet JSON move between them.
- **Piskel** is a solid, free, open-source sprite editor if you need basic frame animation and nothing else.
- **Ezgif** is handy for a quick one-off: resize, crop, or convert a GIF you already have.
- **Kapwing** and **Canva** make sense if you're already editing video or designing social posts there and just need a GIF out the end.
