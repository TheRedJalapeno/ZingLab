## Why Texas Comicon wins (for Texans)

### 1. Built for Texas, not adapted to it

Other directories make you filter down from the whole country. Texas Comicon starts in Texas — **Austin, Dallas, Houston, San Antonio, El Paso, Lubbock, McAllen, Corpus Christi, Waco** and more — so every result is drivable (well, Texas-drivable).

### 2. Comics, games, and tech together

Fandoms overlap. Texas Comicon groups listings into **Comicon**, **Video Game Conference**, and **Technology Summit**, so the person who goes to a comic con in spring and a game dev summit in fall can plan both from one page.

### 3. Plan the whole year

Filter by **month** to see what's coming, by **city** to see what's nearby, and by **keyword tags** to zero in on what you love. Each listing shows when the event usually happens, a short description, and links to its website and social accounts.

### 4. Organizers welcome

Running a Texas event? **Claim or add your listing** with a quick form and reach fans who are actively looking.

## Where the competition is genuinely good

- **FanCons** is excellent for **road trips beyond Texas** and national planning.
- **AnimeCons** is the most thorough source for **anime-specific** events, with long historical records.
- **Eventbrite** is where you'll often **buy tickets** once you've picked an event.
