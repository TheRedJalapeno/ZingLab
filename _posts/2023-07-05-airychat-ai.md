AiryChat is a free, friendly AI chatbot you can try right now. It ships with several quirky personalities out of the box — from professional assistant to playful companion — so you can see different conversation styles and tones in action.

While the public demo is a fun, approachable chatbot for general use, AiryChat also acts as a working showcase for businesses evaluating AI-powered chat, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) integrations, and white-label deployments. The same core can be adapted to power support chatbots, knowledge assistants, or branded conversational experiences that integrate company knowledge bases.

Key features
- Free live demo with multiple personalities and adjustable tone.
- RAG-friendly: connect document stores, FAQs, or knowledge bases to make answers grounded in your own content.
- White-label & integration options for teams that want a branded, hosted chatbot.
- Lightweight API and embeddable widget for websites and internal tools.

Why try the demo
- Compare response styles: toggle personalities to understand how tone affects user engagement.
- Test RAG flows: see how the assistant cites or references uploaded content (where enabled).
- Prototype quickly: use the demo to validate conversational flows before committing to an implementation.

Use cases for businesses
- Customer support: reduce agent load by surfacing FAQ answers and troubleshooting steps.
- Sales enablement: give reps a searchable assistant that draws from playbooks and product docs.
- Internal knowledge: build an internal bot that helps employees find policies, onboarding materials, and technical docs.
- Creative assistants: prototype persona-driven assistants for marketing, education, and entertainment.

Privacy & data handling
AiryChat's public demo is intended for exploration and prototyping. Any data you enter is handled according to the service's privacy policy; for business deployments, data handling and retention policies are configurable and can be tailored to meet compliance needs. When connecting private documents for RAG, production deployments should be configured to keep sensitive data secured within the customer's environment.

Getting started
1. Visit the live demo: the `livelink` in the page header opens the playground.
2. Start a conversation and pick a personality from the options.
3. For business inquiries, contact the team to discuss white-labeling, RAG integrations, and deployment options.

Tips for evaluating
- Try the same prompt across multiple personalities to judge tone and brand fit.
- Feed a few representative documents (product spec, FAQ) to test RAG relevance and source citation.
- Measure latency and fallback behavior for your target deployment (web, mobile, internal tools).

If you're exploring conversational AI for your site or product, AiryChat is an easy way to prototype and demonstrate potential outcomes to stakeholders. The demo is free to try; reach out via the site for custom or enterprise options.
