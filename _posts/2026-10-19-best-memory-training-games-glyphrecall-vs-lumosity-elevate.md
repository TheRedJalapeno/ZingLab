## Why GlyphRecall wins

### 1. It tests the memory you actually use

Repeating something back immediately is easy. Holding it in mind *while doing something else* is the hard part — and the part that matters when you're remembering a phone number while you find a pen. GlyphRecall puts a quick arithmetic problem between study and recall, the same basic structure researchers use in "complex span" working-memory tasks. Right or wrong, you move on; the math is there to occupy you, not to grade you.

### 2. No clock, no stress

Speed and memory are different skills. Timed games blend them together and punish anyone who reads slowly, has a tremor, or is simply nervous. In GlyphRecall you study the glyphs for as long as you like and click "I've got them" when you're ready. That makes it far more welcoming for older adults and anyone in recovery.

### 3. It grows with you

**Progressive** mode starts with 3 glyphs and adds one every 3 levels. **More Progressive** starts at 6 and adds two. Between levels you review the whole set with the new glyph in place. The game always sits right at the edge of your capacity — the zone where practice is most useful.

### 4. Private by default

No account. No email. Name and age are optional. Answers are stored anonymously. And there's an offline mode, so you can play on a plane.

### 5. Free, for real

No trial, no paywall after level 10. If you like it, there's an optional donation link. That's it.

## Where the competition is genuinely good

- **Lumosity** and **Peak** are better if you want **variety** — dozens of games across attention, speed, flexibility, and language.
- **Elevate** is excellent if your goal is sharper **reading, writing, and mental math** rather than raw memory.
- **CogniFit** is designed for **structured cognitive assessment**, often alongside professionals.
- **Human Benchmark** is perfect for a **one-minute score** to compare with friends.

## A five-minute daily routine

1. Open [GlyphRecall](https://glyphrecall.com/?ref=zinglab-blog) and pick **Progressive**.
2. Choose your reason for training (improving memory, rehab & recovery, or just for fun).
3. Play until you miss — that's your level for today. Note it.
4. Bookmark it (Ctrl+D) and come back tomorrow. Try to beat your best, not the leaderboard.
5. When Progressive feels easy, switch to **More Progressive**.

Want to pair it with reading practice? Try a few minutes of [HotGato](/blog/best-speed-reading-app/) afterwards — holding a sentence in mind at speed uses the same muscles.
