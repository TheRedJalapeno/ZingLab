ABOUT

About ZingLab

ZingLab is a small, independent biomedical research organization with a focused mandate: to apply the principles of modern microbiology to practical, translational questions in companion animal health.

We believe that some of the most consequential opportunities in veterinary medicine lie not in novel pharmacology, but in a deeper understanding of the microbial communities that already inhabit our patients. Our research proceeds from the hypothesis that commensal microorganisms, properly characterized and strategically applied, represent a durable and low-risk avenue for disease prevention and quality-of-life improvement in companion animals.

ZingLab operates as a lean, hypothesis-driven laboratory. We prioritize methodological rigor, careful sourcing of biological materials, and collaborative relationships with suppliers, reference culture repositories, and the broader research community.

We are available for research inquiries, supply partnerships, and scientific correspondence.
