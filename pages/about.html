About ZingLab

ZingLab is a small, independent organization with an unfocused mandate: to make the world a better place, and improve the quality of our corner of the internet.

ZingLab operates as a lean, hypothesis-driven laboratory. We prioritize methodological rigor, careful sourcing, and collaborative relationships.

We are available for partnerships, contracts, and other collaborative opportunities.
