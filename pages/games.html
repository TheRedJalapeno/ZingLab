Games

A few games I've developed for fun or educational purposes.

Dungeon of the Mists

A tile-based roguelike dungeon crawler based loosely on Castle of the Winds. Mists has progressive difficulty, creepy lore, charming visuals, cloud saves, and cosmetic add-ons.

Play on OpenFantasy.org

GlyphRecall Memory Trainer

GlyphRecall is a fun short-term memory training game. It pushes your short-term memory capacity alongside retention duration, in a fun and easy to play game.

Play GlyphRecall

To The Victor

Based on the 2014 board game mashup of Settlers of Catan, Risk, and Muchkin. To The Victor is a fast-paced online multiplayer game of conquest.

Play To The Victor

oBalls Physics Game

Simple physics games, one Space Invaders clone, one with a complex audio management system with caching and volume scaling.
