Careers at ZingLab

ZingLab is a growing independent research laboratory focused on practical microbiome science for companion animal health. We hire people who value operational excellence, scientific rigor, collaborative culture, and measurable outcomes.

We believe strong science is built on disciplined operations, clear communication, and a team culture that supports curiosity, accountability, and respect. Candidates should be comfortable working in an agile research environment where cross-functional collaboration and ethical data practices are essential.

Research Assistant – Microbiome & Oral Health

ZingLab is an independent biomedical research laboratory investigating the therapeutic potential of commensal microbial communities in companion animal populations. Operating at the intersection of oral microbiology, probiotic science, and veterinary health outcomes, we source, culture, and characterize microbial strains with the goal of developing evidence-based, microbiome-targeted interventions for underserved patient populations in companion animal medicine. We maintain active university partnerships and run our own in-house laboratory — giving our team the intellectual depth of academia and the pace of an independent research organization. If you want your work to matter, this is where you do it.

Location: Melbourne, Florida

Type: On-site / Hybrid

Schedule: Full-time

Role overview: We are looking for a motivated Research Assistant to join our growing team on an exciting new initiative: a microbiome-based intervention designed to improve dental health and extend the healthy lifespan of companion animals. You will work directly alongside our scientists to culture and characterize microbial strains, design and execute experiments, and generate reproducible data that moves the science forward. This is a hands-on, high-impact role for someone who wants to see their bench work translate into real-world outcomes for pets and their owners.

Key responsibilities:

  • Culture, maintain, and characterize commensal microbial strains under established protocols.
  • Design and execute in vitro experiments to evaluate candidate probiotic formulations targeting oral biofilm communities.
  • Collect, record, and analyze experimental data; maintain rigorous lab notebooks and contribute to research reports.
  • Assist with sample preparation, processing, and sequencing workflows (16S rRNA, metagenomics, or similar).
  • Support laboratory inventory, reagent procurement, and maintenance of equipment and safety standards.
  • Collaborate with university partners and contribute to shared experimental datasets and publications.

Qualifications:

  • B.S. or M.S. in Microbiology, Biology, Biochemistry, Veterinary Science, or a related field.
  • Hands-on laboratory experience; prior work with microbial culture, genomics, or animal health research is a strong plus.
  • Detail-oriented with strong organizational skills and a commitment to reproducible science.
  • Collaborative mindset and genuine curiosity — we value people who ask good questions.

Compensation: Competitive salary commensurate with experience.

Grant Writer

ZingLab is an independent biomedical research laboratory working at the frontier of microbiome science and companion animal health. We partner with universities and maintain our own laboratory to pursue rigorous, translational research — from a digital early-detection tool for cognitive decline to microbiome-based interventions that could add healthy years to the lives of pets worldwide. Our science is ambitious, our team is small and driven, and every role here carries real weight.

Location: Melbourne, Florida

Type: Remote (U.S. preferred)

Schedule: Part-time

Role overview: We are seeking an experienced Grant Writer to help secure the funding that powers our mission. You will take ownership of our grants pipeline — identifying opportunities, crafting compelling narratives, and submitting polished applications to federal agencies, private foundations, and industry partners. You will work closely with our principal investigators to translate complex science into persuasive, fundable proposals. The right person for this role understands that a great grant is as much about storytelling as it is about science.

Key responsibilities:

  • Research and identify federal (NIH, NSF, USDA, and others), foundation, and corporate funding opportunities aligned with ZingLab's research portfolio.
  • Lead the full lifecycle of grant applications: opportunity assessment, narrative development, budget coordination, submission, and post-award reporting.
  • Collaborate with scientists and leadership to synthesize research goals, preliminary data, and impact statements into compelling, accurate proposals.
  • Maintain a grants calendar, track deadlines, and manage application workflows across multiple concurrent submissions.
  • Edit and refine scientific language for clarity, accessibility, and reviewer appeal without sacrificing technical accuracy.
  • Build and maintain relationships with program officers and funding contacts.

Qualifications:

  • 3+ years of grant writing experience, preferably in biomedical, life sciences, or veterinary research contexts.
  • Demonstrated track record of funded proposals (NIH, NSF, or foundation grants strongly preferred).
  • Exceptional writing and editing skills with the ability to translate dense scientific content for diverse audiences.
  • Familiarity with federal grant systems (Grants.gov, eRA Commons, FastLane, or similar).
  • Strong project management skills; comfortable juggling competing deadlines.
  • Background in biology, public health, or a related scientific field is a plus but not required.

Compensation: Competitive salary commensurate with experience.

Senior Partnerships Manager

ZingLab is an independent biomedical research laboratory at the intersection of microbiology, probiotic science, and companion animal health. We operate with the rigor of academia and the agility of a focused, independent lab — partnering with universities, suppliers, and industry leaders to advance research that genuinely improves lives. Our recent projects have ranged from a digital cognitive screening tool for early dementia detection to a microbiome program aimed at extending the healthy lifespan of pets through improved oral health. We move fast, we publish transparently, and we believe the best science happens when the right collaborators are in the room.

Location: Melbourne, Florida

Type: On-site / Remote / Hybrid (U.S. preferred)

Schedule: Full-time / Part-time

Role overview: We are hiring a Senior Partnerships Manager to build and steward the relationships that make our science possible. This is a senior, strategic role that sits at the intersection of business development, research collaboration, and institutional relations. You will identify, cultivate, and manage partnerships with universities, industry partners, reagent and strain providers, contract research organizations, and other stakeholders across the biomedical and veterinary research ecosystem. You'll represent ZingLab externally, negotiate collaboration agreements, and help shape the strategic direction of our partner network.

Key responsibilities:

  • Identify and develop new partnership opportunities with universities, research institutions, industry sponsors, and supplier networks aligned with ZingLab's research agenda.
  • Manage the full partnership lifecycle: outreach, negotiation, agreement execution, onboarding, and ongoing relationship stewardship.
  • Serve as the primary point of contact for institutional and industry collaborators; maintain high-touch, trust-based relationships.
  • Work closely with scientific leadership to align partnership strategy with research priorities and funding goals.
  • Represent ZingLab at conferences, symposia, and stakeholder meetings; build the organization's external profile in the biomedical and veterinary science communities.
  • Develop and track KPIs for the partnerships portfolio; report to leadership on pipeline health, deal progress, and strategic opportunities.
  • Coordinate cross-functionally with grant writing, legal, and research teams to ensure partnerships are structured for mutual success.

Qualifications:

  • 5+ years of experience in partnerships, business development, or strategic alliances — ideally in biomedical research, life sciences, academia-industry collaboration, or a related field.
  • Proven ability to build and close complex partnership arrangements, including research agreements, licensing discussions, or sponsored research deals.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills; comfortable engaging C-suite executives, principal investigators, and university administrators alike.
  • Strategic thinker with strong organizational instincts and the ability to manage a multi-stakeholder portfolio independently.
  • Familiarity with the academic research ecosystem, technology transfer, or veterinary/animal health markets is a significant advantage.
  • Bachelor's degree required; advanced degree in a scientific or business field preferred.

Compensation: Competitive salary commensurate with experience.
