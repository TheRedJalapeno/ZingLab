## Precision bits, full-size control

Phone screens, game controllers, handheld consoles, laptops, and smart-home gadgets are held together with tiny, specialized screws. The 4mm bits in a precision kit fit those screws perfectly. The little handle they come with is a different story: thin, slippery, and hard to turn when a screw has been sitting under factory torque for three years.

**Nested Hex** is a small adapter that lets those 4mm precision bits drop into the standard magnetic screwdriver you already keep in the drawer. Same bit, better handle.

## Built for the bench

- **Phones and tablets:** Pentalobe and tri-point bits through a handle with real leverage.
- **Controllers and handheld consoles:** Tri-Wing and small Phillips, without the cramp.
- **Small appliances and gadgets:** security Torx and odd tips from your kit, in the driver you trust.

Each pack has three adapters, so you can keep one on the bench, one in the toolbox, and one in the kitchen drawer for the TV remote battery door you keep meaning to fix.
