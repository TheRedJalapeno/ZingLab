## Your build has two kinds of screws

The case screws are easy: grab your magnetic screwdriver and go. Then come the M.2 standoff, the laptop bottom panel with eleven tiny Torx, and the fan bracket, and suddenly you're digging for the precision kit with the handle the size of a pencil.

**Nested Hex** ends the swap. It's a small adapter that lets 4mm precision bits fit in your standard magnetic screwdriver, so the whole job happens with one comfortable handle.

## Where it earns its spot

- **M.2 SSDs:** tiny screws, tight spaces, and a magnetic tip that keeps them from vanishing.
- **Laptop panels and batteries:** small Torx and Phillips with enough leverage to crack factory-tight screws without stripping them.
- **Fans, heatsinks, and handhelds:** the odd small fastener your case driver can't reach on its own.
