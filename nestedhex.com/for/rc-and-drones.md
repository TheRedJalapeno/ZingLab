## Your pit bag already has the bits

RC cars, drones, and scale models are full of small screws: motor mounts, servo horns, body posts, and frame plates. Precision kits have the right tips, but their skinny handles are a pain when your hands are cold and the light is fading.

**Nested Hex** lets those 4mm precision bits fit your standard magnetic screwdriver. Use one handle for everything, keep the screw on the magnet, and spend more time driving or flying.

## Where it helps

- **Drones:** frame and motor screws during crash repairs.
- **RC cars and trucks:** body mounts, servo savers, and small hardware.
- **Models and kits:** tiny assembly screws without the hand cramp.
