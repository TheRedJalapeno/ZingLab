## Small screws, big annoyance

A wobbly eyeglass hinge, a watch strap pin screw, a jewelry box clasp, and a loose screw on a pair of sunglasses all need tiny precision bits. The pocket screwdriver that comes free with your glasses usually lasts about one use.

**Nested Hex** lets the 4mm precision bits you already own fit in your standard magnetic screwdriver. You get a handle you can hold steady, a magnet that keeps hold of the screw, and a quick fix at the kitchen table.

## Good for

- **Eyeglasses and sunglasses:** hinge and nose-pad screws.
- **Watches:** strap and bracelet screws on watches that use them.
- **Jewelry and small boxes:** hinges, clasps, and tiny hardware.

Keep the three-pack spread around: one in the junk drawer, one in the travel bag, one at the office.
