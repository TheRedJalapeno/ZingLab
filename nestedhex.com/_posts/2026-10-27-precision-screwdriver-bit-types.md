Modern gadgets are held together by a surprising variety of tiny screws, some of them chosen specifically to keep you out. Here's a quick guide to the precision bits you'll actually meet, and where.

## The common types

### Pentalobe
**Shape:** five rounded lobes, like a flower.
**Where:** Apple iPhones, MacBooks, and Apple Watch, often on the outside screws.
**Tip:** sizes are small and specific (P2 is common on iPhones). Use the exact size.

### Tri-point (Y-type)
**Shape:** three slots in a Y.
**Where:** Apple device internals and some Nintendo hardware.
**Tip:** easy to confuse with Tri-Wing. Look closely.

### Tri-Wing
**Shape:** three offset triangular wings.
**Where:** older Nintendo hardware, some power tools and appliances.

### Torx and security Torx
**Shape:** a six-point star. Security Torx has a pin in the center.
**Where:** laptops, hard drives, game consoles, appliances. Small sizes like T5 and T6 are common in laptops and drives.
**Tip:** for security Torx, you need a bit with a hole in the tip.

### Phillips and JIS
**Shape:** the familiar cross. JIS looks almost identical.
**Where:** nearly everything. Phillips 000, 00, and 0 are common in electronics, and JIS appears on a lot of Japanese-made gear and cameras.
**Tip:** if a "Phillips" screw keeps camming out, try a JIS bit.

### Flat (slotted)
**Where:** eyeglasses, watches, jewelry, older electronics.
**Tip:** choose the widest blade that still fits the slot, for better contact.

### Hex
**Where:** RC cars, drones, bikes, and some small electronics and furniture.

## Quick reference

| Device | Common bits |
|---|---|
| iPhone | Pentalobe (outside), tri-point and Phillips (inside) |
| MacBook | Pentalobe, Torx |
| Windows laptops | Phillips 0/00, small Torx |
| Hard drives and SSD enclosures | Torx T5–T8 |
| Older Nintendo consoles and controllers | Tri-Wing, Phillips |
| Game consoles (various) | Torx, security Torx, Phillips |
| Eyeglasses | Small flat, Phillips 000 |
| RC cars and drones | Hex, Phillips |

Always check a repair guide for your specific model. Manufacturers change screws between generations.
