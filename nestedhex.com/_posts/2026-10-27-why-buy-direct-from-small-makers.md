Nested Hex is designed and made in America by a family-owned business. We'll be on Amazon and Walmart soon, because that's where a lot of people shop. But if you're reading this, here's why **buying direct is best**.

## Where your money goes

When a small maker sells through a big marketplace, the platform usually keeps a significant percentage of every sale as a referral or selling fee. That often comes before fulfillment, advertising, and storage costs. For a small product with a modest price, those fees can easily be larger than what the maker keeps.

When you buy direct:

- **More of the sale goes to the people who made it.**
- **We can keep making things here**, in small batches, in America.
- **You still get free shipping** within the US.
- **Checkout is secure**, handled by Stripe.

## What you're supporting

Nested Hex started as a fix for a small, everyday annoyance: precision bits that don't fit the screwdriver you actually like using. Turning that into a patent-pending, made-in-America product takes design, testing, production, and a lot of packaging tape. Every direct order helps pay for the next batch.

## From our family to yours

Thank you. Whether you're fixing a phone, tightening your glasses, building a PC, or buying a stocking stuffer for the person who fixes everything, we're grateful you chose a small maker.
